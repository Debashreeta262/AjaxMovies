Built around two roles

The portal behaves differently depending on who's signed in — not by asking, but by checking what your account is actually allowed to do.

For employees

Register, sign in, and manage your own profile. You can always see your own details in full.

  • View and keep your own record up to date
  • See the staff directory once an admin turns it on for you
  • Never see admin accounts or admin-only data

For admins

Sign in with an administrator account to see every record and control who can see what.

  • View full details for every employee
  • Grant or revoke directory access per person
  • Suspend or remove accounts

How access works

A short version of the rule the whole system runs on.

Step 1
You register and log in as an employee by default.
Step 2
Your dashboard shows your own record. The directory stays locked.
Step 3
An admin flips your access on. The directory unlocks for you — admin accounts still excluded.
